1. Executive Summary & Purpose

ASO-Hunter is a proprietary, in-house analytical software developed by our mobile application publishing team. The tool is designed to monitor, analyze, and optimize App Store Optimization (ASO) metrics for our self-published mobile wellness and productivity applications on Google Play and Apple App Store.

The primary goal of integrating the Google Ads API is to evaluate real-world user search volume and keyword demand via Google's KeywordPlanIdeaService, helping our marketing and product teams identify relevant, high-demand, and non-saturated search queries to include in mobile application titles, subtitles, and descriptions.

Audience & Compliance Notice: This tool is strictly restricted to internal company staff (mobile marketing managers, ASO specialists, and data analysts). It is not offered as a commercial SaaS, not distributed to external clients, and no data retrieved from the Google Ads API is ever resold, distributed, or exposed to third parties.

2. Google Ads API Usage & Technical Integration

Our integration relies exclusively on read-only keyword planning capabilities:

  • Service: KeywordPlanIdeaService
  • Endpoint Method: customers/{customer_id}:generateKeywordIdeas
  • Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords
  • Input Parameters:
    • language: target storefront language (e.g., languageConstants/1000 for English, 1031 for Russian).
    • geoTargetConstants: target country criterion ID (e.g., geoTargetConstants/2840 for United States).
    • keywordPlanNetwork: GOOGLE_SEARCH
    • keywordSeed: the list of mobile application search queries currently tracked by our team.
  • Retrieved Data: Monthly average search volumes (avgMonthlySearches), competition index, and related keyword ideas.

5. Security & Privacy Safeguards

  • Authentication: All requests utilize standard Google OAuth 2.0 with secure offline refresh tokens stored in encrypted environment variables.
  • Data Retention: Only aggregated metric numbers (monthly volumes) are stored in our private relational database for tracking historical trends.
  • Confidentiality: No personally identifiable information (PII) is involved in keyword planning queries.
